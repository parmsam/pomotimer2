# Quotes

The default quotes shown under the timer when **Settings → Quotes** is on.

**Rules for this file**

- One quote per blockquote. The last line is the attribution: `— Author, *Source* (year)`.
- Only well-sourced quotes. Misattributions are everywhere (Einstein, Edison and Twain especially), so check the person's Wikiquote "Sourced" section or Quote Investigator. Anything listed as misattributed or disputed doesn't go in.
- Keep them about work, focus, learning, curiosity, time and persistence, and keep them short enough to read at a glance.
- `npm test` checks that every entry parses, has an author, and isn't duplicated.
